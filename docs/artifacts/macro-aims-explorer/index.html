The Four + Two Aims

The macroeconomic objectives every UK government juggles at once, why they matter, and why hitting one often means missing another.

The big idea

Every UK government has four main aims (economic growth, low unemployment, stable prices, a balance of payments that is not in persistent deficit) plus two more (a fair distribution of income and protecting the environment). Hitting one aim often means missing another.

Exam-style questions

Top tips

  • Name the aim, then define it. "Real GDP growth" is not the same as "GDP rising".
  • Use the UK context. Mention the ONS, the Bank of England, CPI and the 2% target.
  • Show the conflict. Say which other aim is hurt and why: growth can cause inflation and a worse current account.
  • Evaluation answers need both sides and a judgement. Say what it depends on.
  • Read the data question carefully. Quote years and figures from the chart.
